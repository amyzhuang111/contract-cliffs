This Master Hosting Services Agreement
(the “Master HSA”) is entered into on this 4th day of April, 2023 by and between REBEL MINING COMPANY, LLC
a limited liability corporation organized and existing under the laws of Wyoming, having its principal offices at 680 S. Cache St., Suite

certain hosting services to digital currency data center clients (the “Managed Services”) including rack space, network
services, electrical connections, routine facility maintenance, and technical support;

describing the Client Equipment to be installed at the Host Facility, the term, commencement date, required pre-paid services, fees,
and payment schedule; and,
WHEREAS Client acknowledges and
agrees that this Master HSA shall govern any and all Hosting Orders with Host.
