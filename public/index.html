Act by

Soonest decision first. Act-by = end of the current term minus the notice period in the clause. Items that need review sit below the dated ones; dates already past sit last.

    Method

    Extraction is deterministic: regular expressions plus date and duration parsing (“sixty (60) days”, “one hundred and eighty (180) days”, “Eighty-Four (84) months”). No language model and no network call touches your documents; uploads stay in this browser tab. A term of N months “commencing on” a date ends the day before the anniversary; “until N years following” ends on the anniversary. When words and numerals disagree, or a value is redacted, the field is left blank and marked for review.

    Accuracy, stated plainly: tuned on the 10 contracts above (all hand-checked). On 14 further EDGAR Exhibit 10 agreements it had never seen, the first pass got 2 of 14 act-by dates right, flagged 5 for review correctly, missed 6 where the clause was readable, and gave 1 wrong date (since fixed). After rule fixes: 8 dated (3 of those still marked for review), 6 correctly left blank and marked, 0 wrong. Those 14 now count as tuning data, not a test (details).

    Real contracts are Exhibit 10 material agreements filed with the SEC, fetched from EDGAR with a declared User-Agent under SEC fair-access rules. Dates projected past the initial term assume the agreement was not terminated or amended. Not legal advice.
